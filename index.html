BAT — Veteran Navigation BETA

Pathfinder

A navigator for veterans. Tell us your situation. We find the right path through VA programs, housing, healthcare, legal aid, and more.

What this is

The VA system is complicated on purpose. Eligibility rules change, programs overlap, and the path from "I need help" to "here is the resource" is rarely clear. Pathfinder is a guided intake system that takes your situation — branch, discharge status, housing, disability, what you're dealing with — and routes you to the specific programs, offices, and contacts that apply.

It doesn't give legal or medical advice. It doesn't store your data on any server. It tells you where to go and who to call.

Coverage areas
VA Benefits & Claims Disability ratings, PACT Act burn pit presumptives, previously denied claims, appeals, TDIU, SMC, Aid & Attendance.
Housing HUD-VASH, SSVF rapid re-housing, DV housing, criminal record barriers, VA home loan, eviction defense, adaptive housing grants.
Healthcare & Mental Health VA enrollment, VAMC assignment, Vet Center referral, MST care, caregiver support, Crisis Line routing.
Legal Discharge upgrade, VA appeals, MST legal support, civilian legal aid, records correction, predatory lending.
Business & Employment SDVOSB/VOSB certification, federal contracting, SBA programs, Voc Rehab Ch. 31, employment transition.
Transportation, Women Veterans, Toxic Exposure BTSSS travel pay, DAV transport, rural transport, Agent Orange, Camp Lejeune, Gulf War Syndrome, PFAS — plus women-specific care tracks.
Beta status

Limited access, Western Slope Colorado pilot. Pathfinder is under active development. We are testing with a small group of veterans across different branches, discharge statuses, and situations before expanding.

The system is honest about what it doesn't know. When local data for your area isn't confirmed, it says so. When a case is unusual, it flags it and tells you to verify with a VSO before acting. It will not route you toward a program you don't qualify for and call it complete.

National expansion is the goal. This is the pilot that gets it right first.
